# Figure assets

Publication figures live here, separately from experiment outputs in `RUNS/`.
The initial assets were generated by the plotting utilities under
`SRC/scripts/` and selected from the local paper workspace.

- `fig1_repr_room.png` compares RGB and depth from mesh, NeRF, and 3DGS.
- `fig2_case_study.{pdf,png}` shows the 3DGS-depth/downsampling case study.
- `fig2a_gs_depth_crop.png` is the depth-detail crop used by the case study.
- `fig3_fidelity_control.{pdf,png}` relates rendering fidelity and pose error.

Prefer PDF for plots in LaTeX and PNG for raster image grids. Regenerate a
figure from benchmark outputs when its underlying result changes, and keep the
caption, units, and provenance synchronized with the manuscript.
